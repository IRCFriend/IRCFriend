IRCFriend support

Questions are welcome.

Have questions, comments, or bug reports? Please reach out. Support contact details will be published before IRCFriend is released.

Help

Getting started

For connection help, have the IRC server address, port, nickname, room name, and TLS setting available. IRCFriend’s in-app help also explains connections, private messages, TLS, and DCC file-transfer safety.

Feedback

Bug reports

When reporting a problem, please include the macOS version, IRCFriend version, the steps that led to the problem, and any error text. Please remove passwords, private keys, private messages, and other sensitive information before sharing screenshots or logs.

For information about how IRCFriend handles data, read the Privacy by Design page.
